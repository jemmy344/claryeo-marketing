How this is worked out

Your employer deducts PAYE monthly and remits it to your State Internal Revenue Service by the 10th of the following month. The mechanics, reliefs and filing deadlines are covered in our complete PAYE guide. If you are self-employed rather than salaried, see how freelancers pay tax in Nigeria.
