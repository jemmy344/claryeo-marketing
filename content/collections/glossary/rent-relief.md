## Worked example

You pay ₦1,500,000 a year in rent. 20% of that is ₦300,000, which is below the ₦500,000 cap, so you claim **₦300,000**. On a ₦6,000,000 salary, that cuts taxable income to ₦5,700,000 and saves ₦54,000 of tax at the 18% band.

Pay ₦3,000,000 rent and 20% would be ₦600,000, but the cap limits your claim to **₦500,000**.

## What changed

Before 2026, the main personal relief was the [Consolidated Relief Allowance](/glossary/cra), calculated as the higher of ₦200,000 or 1% of gross income, plus 20% of gross income. The 2026 rules replaced that with rent relief, so guidance written for earlier years no longer describes the current position.
