## When you will be asked for one

- Bidding for government or large corporate contracts
- Applying for certain business licences and permits
- Some visa applications
- Property transactions and large financial dealings

## How to get one

Apply to the authority you file with: your State Internal Revenue Service for individuals, FIRS for companies. You must have filed returns and paid what is due for the last three years, so a TCC application is often when unfiled years surface.

## The practical lesson

Businesses that only think about tax when a contract requires a TCC find themselves unable to bid, because clearing three years of arrears takes time. Filing on time each year keeps the certificate a formality.
