## Federal or state: which one do you deal with?

| Tax | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Company Income Tax (CIT) | FIRS |
| VAT | FIRS |
| Withholding tax on company payments | FIRS |
| Personal Income Tax / PAYE | State Internal Revenue Service |

Most small businesses deal with both: FIRS for the company's own taxes, and the state authority for their employees' PAYE.

## Which state?

PAYE goes to the state where the **employee** resides, not where the company is registered. Employers with staff across several states remit to several authorities.

The FIRS remit is being carried forward under the reorganised revenue service established by the 2025 tax reforms; the practical split between federal and state taxes above is unchanged.
