## Worked example

You invoice a client ₦1,000,000 for taxable services. You add 7.5% VAT of **₦75,000**, so the invoice total is ₦1,075,000. You collect the ₦75,000 on behalf of FIRS and remit it, less any input VAT you paid on business purchases that month.

## What is exempt

Basic food items, medical supplies, educational materials and exported goods are VAT-exempt. If you sell a mix of exempt and taxable items, your invoice must show the split.

## If you are not registered

Businesses below the ₦25 million threshold must not charge or show VAT on invoices. Charging VAT without being registered is a compliance problem, not a shortcut.
