## Who files this way

Freelancers, consultants, sole proprietors and anyone earning income that is not salaried employment. If you have both a salary and freelance income, PAYE covers the salary and direct assessment covers the rest.

## The deadline

Annual returns are due by **31 March** of the following year, filed on Form A with your state authority.

## What you need

- A summary of total income for the year
- Your allowable business expenses and reliefs
- Evidence of any advance tax or [WHT](/glossary/wht) already paid
- Your [TIN](/glossary/tin)

## The practical advice

Set aside 20-25% of each invoice as it is paid. Direct assessment means nobody is deducting tax for you, so the full year's bill arrives at once. The [freelancer tax guide](/guides/freelancer-tax-nigeria) walks through the whole process.
