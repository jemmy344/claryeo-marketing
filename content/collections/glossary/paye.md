## Who it applies to

Every employee earning above the national minimum wage of ₦70,000 a month (₦840,000 a year). Your employer calculates the deduction, takes it from your gross pay, and remits it by the 10th of the following month.

## Worked example

On a gross salary of ₦500,000 a month (₦6,000,000 a year), PAYE under the 2026 bands is **₦72,500 a month**, leaving ₦427,500 take-home, an effective rate of 14.5%. See the [full band-by-band breakdown](/tax-calculator/500k-monthly).

## Current position

PAYE is governed by the Personal Income Tax Act, with the band structure updated by the Nigeria Tax Act for 2026: 0% on the first ₦800,000 of taxable income, rising to 25% above ₦50,000,000.

Self-employed people do not pay through PAYE. They file under direct assessment instead, covered in the [freelancer tax guide](/guides/freelancer-tax-nigeria).
