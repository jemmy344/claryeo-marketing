## How to get one

**Individuals and sole proprietors:** register with the State Internal Revenue Service of the state where you live. Bring valid ID (NIN, driver's licence or passport), proof of address, and your business name registration if you have one.

**Companies:** register through the FIRS Joint Tax Board portal or a FIRS office, with your CAC registration certificate, memorandum of association, utility bill and valid ID.

## Why freelancers need one too

A TIN is not just for companies. If you earn freelance income in Nigeria you are required to register and file, even if you also have a salaried job where PAYE is already deducted.

There is no charge for registering.
