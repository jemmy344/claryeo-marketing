## Worked example

An employee on exactly ₦70,000 a month pays **no PAYE** and takes home the full ₦70,000. An employee on ₦100,000 a month is above the threshold and pays ₦5,000 a month, an effective rate of 5%.

## How it interacts with the 0% band

Separately from the exemption, the first ₦800,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0% under the 2026 bands. This is why tax rises gently rather than jumping at the threshold: someone just above minimum wage still pays very little.
