Think about your hourly rate. Now multiply it by the five hours you spent last weekend
designing invoices, tracking down old bank receipts, and doing maths on your phone.
You aren't just doing paperwork, you're losing revenue.

## The myth of free admin

Solopreneurs treat administrative tasks as free time rather than non-billable
overhead. It isn't free. It's the most expensive work you do, because nobody is
paying for it.

## Calculating your drag coefficient

Take your billable rate, multiply it by the hours you spend on manual tracking each
month, and you have the income that admin quietly costs you. Most people are
surprised by the number.

## The mental tax

Switching between the work you're actually good at and spreadsheet management carries
a cost that doesn't show up in hours at all. It shows up as burnout.
