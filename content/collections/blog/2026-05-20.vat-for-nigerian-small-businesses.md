Value Added Tax (VAT) trips up a lot of Nigerian founders, not because it is
complicated, but because nobody explains it plainly. Here is the short version.

## Do you even need to charge VAT?

If your business is registered and your taxable turnover crosses the threshold,
you charge 7.5% VAT on most goods and services and remit it to FIRS. Plenty of
small businesses register voluntarily too, because it makes you look established
to larger clients.

## What you actually have to do

- Add 7.5% VAT to the right line items on your invoices.
- Keep a clean record of VAT collected (output) and VAT paid on expenses (input).
- File and remit the difference to FIRS, on time, every month.
